Experience values (Table 1): [1, 3, 3, 4, 5, 5, 8, 10, 12, 15, 15, 20, 21] → sum 122, n = 13, mean = 122/13 = **9.3846**. The paper reports 9.35. Rounding and truncation both give 9.38, so 9.35 cannot come from Table 1 as printed. Most likely a typo, or one Table 1 value differs from the data used for the text. The median (8) matches.

## 4. Checking the paper's qualitative claim with its own Table B1

Claim (Abstract / Discussion): developers *favoured in-context (semi-formal and informal) learning*.

| Learning type | Activities | Mentions | Share | Distinct participants |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Formal | 4 | 11 | 21% | 7 |
| Semi-formal | 5 | 21 | 40% | 10 |
| Informal | 3 | 20 | 38% | 11 |

Semi-formal + informal = 41/52 mentions (79%) vs formal 11/52. The table is **consistent** with the claim, although B1 counts *mentions*, not preferences.

## 6. What cannot be reproduced

- The coding (170 codes / 600 excerpts) and the themes: interview transcripts are not published.
- Table C1 (motivation codes) gives definitions and quotes, not counts.
- Hence the DT074 project reproduces the paper **selectively**: its findings become design requirements and testable hypotheses (docs/PAPER_MAPPING.md), measured on NIST SARD.
